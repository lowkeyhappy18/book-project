Chapter 8: Information Security and Cyber Law

Information security is the practice of protecting computer systems, networks and digital information from unauthorized access, misuse, damage or loss. In the digital society, information security is important for maintaining the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information. Cybercrimes such as hacking, phishing, identity theft, malware attacks and online fraud can cause serious damage to individuals and organizations. Security measures such as strong passwords, antivirus software, firewalls, encryption and regular data backups help protect information from various threats.

Cyber law refers to the laws and regulations that govern the use of computers, digital devices, networks and the Internet. It deals with activities such as cybercrime, digital signatures, intellectual property rights and the responsible use of digital information. Copyrights, patents and trademarks help protect the rights of creators and organizations over their digital works and ideas. Cyber law and information security together promote safe, responsible and ethical use of technology and help protect people and organizations in the digital environment.
