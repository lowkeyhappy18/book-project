Chapter 7: Multimedia

Multimedia is the combination of different forms of media such as text, graphics, audio, video and animation to present information in an interactive and meaningful way. It is widely used in education, entertainment, business, communication and advertising. Multimedia makes information more attractive and easier to understand by presenting content through different types of media instead of using text alone.

Text and graphics are used to display written information and visual elements, while audio and video provide sound and moving visual information. Animation creates the appearance of movement by displaying a series of images or objects in sequence. Digital multimedia can be created and edited using various software tools and is commonly found in websites, presentations, games, educational programs and digital advertisements. Thus, multimedia plays an important role in modern communication and information presentation.
